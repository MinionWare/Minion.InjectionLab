# About Minionware

Minion.InjectionLab is a free teaching tool from **Minionware** — click the
Minionware logo at the top of the left nav, or `Minionware > minionware.net`
in the menu bar, to visit **[minionware.net](https://minionware.net)**
directly from the app.

More Minionware products will show up under `Minionware > Other products`
as they're ready — that menu item is a placeholder for now.
